A focus/avoid conflict on the same area (allowed by `AcademicInterestProfile`'s own contract — focus/avoid are independent lists) shows up fully in BOTH `matchedFocusAreas` and `avoidedAreas`, never hidden, even when they cancel out in the final clamped score.
